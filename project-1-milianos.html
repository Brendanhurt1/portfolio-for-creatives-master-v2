A restaurant experience where everyone is family.

Miliano’s is a welcoming Little Italy restaurant on Commercial Drive that celebrates authentic Italian culture and community through family-oriented long-table dining where strangers can become friends. They required a visual identity that reflected their genuine care for their Italian heritage and their Vancouver community. They required a brand guide, menu design, as well as collateral packaging and coasters to expand their brand beyond the restaurant.

The menu uses Italian mosaic tile shapes with graphic textures from photos taken on Commercial Drive. Every wall, brick, and mural is reflected in the design, with each menu color-coded for easy distinction. Brand characters appear throughout, representing specific dishes. The in-restaurant pasta sauce trio brings together unexpected ingredient pairings that work perfectly—just like the unlikely people sharing the long table, where your perfect pairing might be a stranger across from you.
