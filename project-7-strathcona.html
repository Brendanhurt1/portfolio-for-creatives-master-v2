Strathcona Post

Strathcona Post is a community-driven campaign inviting visitors to rediscover Vancouver’s oldest residential neighbourhood. Through a free, interactive stamp-collecting experience, the campaign celebrates Strathcona’s rich history, heritage homes, and local businesses while encouraging visitors to explore the area in a new light.

Inspired by traditional Japanese stamp collecting, Strathcona Post blends playful illustrations with a nostalgic, hands-on experience. Its colourful identity brings the neighbourhood’s stories to life, encouraging exploration, community connection, and a chance to disconnect from the digital world.

The concept for this project was made in collaboration with Rachel Lu and Keira Schick. Logo designed by Rachel Lu.

Simple Stamping Stations

The social media reel brings Strathcona’s history to life, featuring preserved heritage and figures like Nora Hendrix’s house—Jimi Hendrix’s grandmother, where Jimi once spent his summers. The main character was illustrated in Illustrator and animated in After Effects, where refining the walk cycle taught me the essentials of motion design.
