West Point Brewing

West Point Brewing is a Vancouver-based brewery crafting beer, sours, and cider infused with local BC ingredients like blackberries and cedar. Rooted in the flavours of the Pacific Northwest, the brand celebrates the journey from nature to beverage through a playful, illustrative identity.

Inspired by BC’s landscapes and local ingredients, West Point Brewing brings its unique brewing process to life through colourful, story-driven design. Its cohesive visual identity blends handcrafted charm with a sense of adventure, creating a brand that feels distinctly local, approachable, and full of character.

Social Media Motion

Social media these days is by far the most effective way to reach a targeted, but massive audience easily and get the most eyes on an expanding business. To tie it all together, a social media reel showcases the brewing process and brewery locations, formatted for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts with safe margins for on-screen text.
