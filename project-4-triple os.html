Triple O's

Triple O's, as part of their 2026 rebrand required a complete brand refresh, including social media advertising on multiple platforms. Unveiling returning favourites, the social media utilized the ryhthem of the music to showcase the various meal options overlayed on the flashy new brand colours.

During my time with the talented people at PS&Co group, I was tasked with creating a multi stage social media advertising campaign as part of the broader brand refresh that the studio handled.

Social Media Campaign
Motion Design

i. A delicious youtube advertisement

For the Youtube advertisement, we only had a limited six seconds to showcase some of the numerous options in the Triple O's lineup and give a moment for the brand's logo to rest.

ii. supported by an instagram story and post

On the left, the Instagram story takes a familiar trend of rapidly cycling options, in this case, Triple O's menu options. The user is encouraged to tap and hold their screen therefore pausing the cycle and revealing their randomly selected delicious option and find their delicious moment.

On the right, a vertical format Instagram Post features a similar concept to the Youtube ad but at a slower tempo allowing more room for each meal option to breathe.
